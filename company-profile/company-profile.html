Who We Are

An engineering enterprise trusted for demanding work

Founded in Nagpur in 2014, Sunrise Constructions has grown into a ₹115 crore+ engineering and construction enterprise delivering landmark infrastructure with institutional rigor.

We work with prestigious clients including NHAI, NH-PWD, and the Irrigation Department — delivering highway packages, major bridges, lift irrigation schemes, residential colonies, and healthcare facilities across Maharashtra.

Our team of 250+ engineers, project managers, and skilled tradespeople combines modern project controls with hands-on field expertise. The result: disciplined execution, transparent reporting, and infrastructure built to perform for generations.
